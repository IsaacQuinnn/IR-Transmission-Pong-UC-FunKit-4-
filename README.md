Once Microcontroller is plugged in and working, "PRESS TO PLAY" should appear on the screen.
This means the controller is working.

Orientate the Microcontroller so the text is upright and facing you, and so the joystick is in the bottom left of the controller.

Who ever wants to start presses the NAVSWITCH down.

To move the paddle either move the NAVSWITCH NORTH or SOUTH (left and right)

To win, try to get the other player to loose the ball, e.g the ball goes pass their paddle.
Make sure the ball does not go pass your paddle or you will lose.

If someone wins "PLAY AGAIN?" will appear on the screen.
To play again, someone presses the NAVSWITCH down.
To stop playing, unplug the Microcontroller.
